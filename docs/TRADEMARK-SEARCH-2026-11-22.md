Not legal advice. This is a knockout search of the public USPTO and CIPO databases for the exact word "navi" (USPTO: 387 hits, CIPO: 208 hits). It does not cover common-law use, app-store names, domains, or confusingly similar marks (Navvi, Navy, Naavi...). A Canadian trademark agent should run the full clearance before any filing.

## Read
- "Navi" is crowded. No exact NAVI in a pure maps/navigation app class was found on either register, which is good.
- The risk is Interactiv's NAVI (driving/mobile) and SKYNAV's NAVI (travel software). A distinctive logo and the app name "Navi Pacenotes" or similar would help differentiate but does not remove the risk.
- Cheaper to decide now than after launch: either keep "Navi" with a qualifier (for example "Navi Rally") or pick a rarer name.
