# Navi Pro offer sheet, 25 Nov 2026

For you to decide. Nothing here is live and no payment flow exists.

## Objection first

You asked for 40 to 75 USD a year. The market report found rivals at 30 to 60 a year (Kurviger 29.99 EUR, REVER 39.99 USD, Rods 36 to 45 USD, Calimoto 59.99 EUR), and the loudest complaint in reviews is paywalled basics. So 40 to 75 works only if the free app stays complete and Pro is clearly extra. The report's own suggestion was 20 to 30 USD; that was a judgement, not tested. Price below is inside your range, and the first test should be a waitlist, not a store listing.

## Free forever (never paywalled)

Live calls, all dialects, free roam, GPX import, the note editor, corrections, hazard calls, roundabout and junction calls, one fun loop at a time, one offline region, game mode, share links, no account. Hazard calls stay free because they are safety information.

## Pro: what it could include

| Pro feature | Status today | Why a driver pays |
|---|---|---|
| Unlimited whole-region offline packs | **Built**, free tier limited to one region in the app (limit not yet coded) | Offline is a top request; big downloads cost real server load |
| Fun loop pro: several loop options, length slider, "avoid this road" list | Basic loop **built**; extras not built | Saves the hour spent in a planner |
| GPX export with the pacenotes as waypoints, share a stage | **Built** | Take stages to other devices and friends |
| Premium voices (recorded Michael / Emma / Heart packs) | Not done | Voice is the product; this is the most visible upgrade |
| Convoy view (live location for a group, no account) | Not built, needs a small server | Strong growth lever, top group-ride request |
| Cloud sync of saved roads and stages | Not built | Plan on a big screen, drive on the phone |
| CarPlay and Android Auto | Not built, needs native apps and platform review | Decides who wins; probably free, since paywalling it caused review backlash elsewhere |

## Suggested price points (inside your range)

- **Pro yearly: 49 USD.** Below Calimoto, above Kurviger, matches Rods at the top.
- **Pro one-time: 75 USD.** Stands out against subscription anger; top of your range.
- Club licence: free for club events, from the growth plan.

## Honest read

- Today Pro would hold only offline packs, loop extras and GPX export. That is thin for 49 a year. Premium voices and convoy are what make it feel worth it, and neither is done.
- Do not charge before the free app has real users. Run a waitlist: measure sign-ups per 100 planned routes.
- Apple and Google stores take a share of in-app sales (check current rates before pricing).

## Decisions for you

1. Yearly only, one-time only, or both?
2. Which Pro items to build first: premium voices or convoy?
3. Waitlist first, or build payments after launch?
