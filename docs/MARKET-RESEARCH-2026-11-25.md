# Navi: market research, 25 Nov 2026

Research window: 25 Nov 2026, 12:53 AM to about 2:30 AM ET. Method: web search plus page fetches (app-store listings and reviews, vendor support threads, forums, trade press, analyst summaries).

## Read this first: what could not be reached

- **Reddit and TikTok could not be read.** Reddit is blocked for the web-fetch tool and for the user's Chrome. TikTok comments came back empty. So there are **no Reddit or TikTok quotes in this report**, and none were made up.
- Driver wishes come from: app-store reviews, a long Calimoto support-forum thread, Kurviger and Apple developer forums, Rennlist/ADVrider/BMW MOA/Miata-club forum snippets, and trade press (Android Authority, BGR, SlashGear, Jalopnik, Car and Driver).
- The pacenote apps are so new that most have fewer than 500 ratings. Their "driver wishes" are thin. The strongest evidence is for bike route apps (Calimoto, Kurviger, REVER), and for subscriptions in general.
- Labels used below: **[driver]** = said by a user in a review or thread. **[listing]** = from the app's own page. **[press]** = a journalist or analyst. **[read]** = Claude's own judgement.

## The headline

1. **Navi is not first any more.** In 2025-26 at least eight apps arrived that read corners aloud on public roads. The closest is **Rods** (iPhone and Android, CarPlay, rally 1-6 calls, free roam). **Tornante** and **Sideways** (iPhone only) also grade corners 1-6. **AllAuto** adds convoys. [listing]
2. **Nobody owns the "find a fun road, then have it called to you" job.** Rods' own blog tells drivers to use Calimoto to find roads and Rods to call them, which means two apps. [listing] That gap is Navi's best opening.
3. **Subscriptions are the loudest complaint.** Drivers push back on paywalls in Calimoto, Kurviger voice, and Rods live calls. A free core is a real selling point. [driver]
4. **Android is under-served for pacenotes.** Tornante and Sideways list iPhone only. Rods and GRIP Note are the Android names. [listing]
5. **CarPlay and Android Auto decide who wins on bikes and cars.** Calimoto's Android Auto thread ran for years with riders threatening to cancel. [driver]

## Market size (analyst numbers, treat as rough)

- Motorcycle navigation systems: USD 392.2 M in 2025, forecast USD 827.6 M by 2034 (8.6% a year). Mostly devices, not apps. [press: Fortune Business Insights]
- Calimoto says 3 million+ users. Porsche's ROADS app had about 180,000 users when it was relaunched. [listing/press]
- CarPlay or Android Auto is on more than 80% of late-model cars, so a phone app can reach the car screen. [press: Mordor Intelligence]
- App-only revenue for "driving enthusiast" navigation is not published anywhere found. Do not quote a total market for Navi.

## What drivers say they want, ranked by how well it is backed

1. **Do not paywall the basics.**
   - [driver] A Calimoto reviewer called the price "ridiculous" and moved to a free rival. Another said GPX import works but cannot be used without the subscription.
   - [driver] Rods reviews: "Need paid app to use it" and "requires a subscription".
   - [press] Flitsmeister got a wave of 1-star reviews for putting CarPlay and Android Auto behind a paywall.
2. **CarPlay and Android Auto.** [driver] Calimoto riders wrote that they would not renew without Android Auto, and one was switching phone brands over it. Porsche ROADS reviewer wanted CarPlay. Rods reviewers praise its CarPlay.
3. **Work with the screen off, without draining the battery.** [listing] Sideways, Pacenote and Rods all headline background audio. [press] One round-up lists Calimoto's battery use as a con.
4. **Free roam (no planned route).** [driver] An Android Rods reviewer says what hooked them was that "you don't need a route". It calls corners and junctions as you drive.
5. **Offline that really works.** [listing] Rods and Sideways sell "download once". [driver/forum] A Kurviger user reports it will not recalculate off-route offline. [press] Waze cannot reroute without a connection.
6. **Quieter, clearer voice.** [press] Waze added a "Less Chatty" mode after years of complaints about interruptions. [press] Google Maps audio garbles local street names.
7. **Keep my route.** [press] A writer says Google keeps trying to reroute them away from a chosen route to save five minutes.
8. **Plan on a bigger screen, then drive on the phone, and drag the route.** [driver] Rennlist, Apple forum and Ferrari forum posts ask for exactly this, with many waypoints.
9. **Group rides and convoys.** [listing] A dozen new apps (TwoTired, Group Ride, Packt Moto, Cardo, RISER, AllAuto) pitch live location because WhatsApp "explodes" on a ride. Thin on reviews, so demand is plausible but unproven.
10. **Bike basics.** [press] Helmet Bluetooth audio, big glove-friendly controls, and bike displays over Android Auto (Sygic, Calimoto on Chigee).
11. **Accuracy of timing.** [driver] One Rods reviewer saw the car placed about 50 m behind. Calls arrive late if GPS lags.
12. **Privacy.** [listing] Tornante advertises no account, no ads, no tracking. [driver] A RISER reviewer wants private profiles.

## Safety and legal notes

- Several rivals warn "off-road or closed course only" (Pacenote) or "not a navigation system, calls may be wrong, late or missing" (Tornante). Navi already has a safety gate. Keep it, and keep the wording plain. [listing]
- Speed-camera alerts are popular on Waze and Rods, but they are restricted in some countries. This is Claude's general knowledge, not checked in this research. If added, make it region-aware and off by default, and check each country's law first.
- Do not add scores, ranks or "personal best speed" for public roads. It rewards speeding and invites app-store and press trouble. [read]

## Platform rules that shape the plan

- **CarPlay:** an app needs a CarPlay entitlement from Apple. A navigation entitlement is for apps designed primarily to provide navigation, and cannot be combined with audio or messaging features in one app. Apple reviews each request, and one developer reports being ignored. [Apple docs, developer forum] Requires a native iPhone app, not a web page.
- **Android Auto:** needs the Android for Cars App Library and passes a Google Play car-quality review (templates, contrast, voice commands). [Google docs] Also requires a native app.
- **So:** a web app (PWA) cannot reach the car screen. The native builds already on the roadmap are the doorway. [read]

## What needs to be in Navi

### Already in Navi (keep, market-validated)
Corner grades 1-6 with rally, reversed and plain wording; editable notes; Repeat / Tighter / Easier / Sooner / Later corrections; Stage view; GPX file load; route options (avoid motorways, tolls, ferries, stops); units; early-call setting; roundabout, junction and caution calls; share links; saved places; prepared voice and the home PC voice; no account.

### Must have (strongest evidence)
1. **Free core.** Live calls, all dialects, GPX and the editor stay free. Charge only for extras (see pricing). [driver complaints]
2. **Native iPhone and Android apps** with reliable screen-off audio and low battery use. Needed for items 3 and 5 too.
3. **CarPlay and Android Auto.** Needs Apple entitlement request and Google car review. Start the Apple request early; it can be slow.
4. **Free roam.** Calls corners on whatever road you are on, with no route. Largest single "delight" signal.
5. **Whole-region offline download** (not only places already viewed). Today Navi only keeps tiles for viewed places.
6. **Find a fun road, then drive it with calls, in one app.** A "fun loop near me" generator with a twist score. This is the gap rivals leave open.
7. **A quiet default.** Calls only, no chatter, clear voice. Navi's voice work already points here.

### Should have
8. GPX **export** and share of notes (import already exists).
9. Hazard calls: speed bumps, dips, surface changes, narrowings. Speed limit on screen.
10. Languages: Tornante lists 22, Rods is English-first. Start with UI text and units for German, French, Italian, Spanish.
11. Bluetooth and helmet-audio check, bigger controls mode for gloves.
12. Convoy view (live location for a group, opt-in, no account). Strong growth lever, but needs a small server.
13. "Keep my route" behaviour: never reroute away from a chosen road without asking. Verify what Navi does today.

### Skip or later
- Social feed, garage and events (AllAuto): heavy, needs moderation, off-mission.
- Professional co-driver notebooks and rally-time tools (Rally Codriver sells at up to 199.99 USD a year): different buyer.
- Lap timers and circuit mode (Tornante beta): niche; revisit after launch.
- Ads, forced accounts, driving scores and speed leaderboards: avoid.

## Pricing idea (for the owner to decide)

- Benchmarks seen: Kurviger 29.99 EUR a year, REVER 39.99 USD, Rods 36-45 USD, Calimoto 59.99 EUR. [listing]
- Suggested: everything a driver needs on a drive is free. Optional Pro about 20-30 USD a year, or a one-time purchase, for convoys, region packs, extra voices, and cloud sync. A one-time option stands out against the subscription anger. [read, not tested]

## How to reach the most drivers (read, not tested)

- Android first-class from day one while rivals are iPhone only.
- Road cards and share links that open in the app: every shared fun road is an ad.
- A Discord for feedback (Rods' reviewers mention theirs).
- Short videos of a real stage with the calls playing.
- Clubs and track-day groups: free licence for club events (already in the growth plan).
