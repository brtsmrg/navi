- **Home PC voice** (optional, off by default; `tools/home-voice/`): runs on the driver's own computer with Chatterbox TTS (MIT; Resemble AI; https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox), PyTorch (BSD-3-Clause), FastAPI and Uvicorn (MIT/BSD), and the Cloudflare quick tunnel `cloudflared` (Apache 2.0; https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflared) to give the phone an HTTPS link. Chatterbox adds an inaudible Perth watermark to its audio. Speech it makes is AI-generated.
- **Premium voice** (optional, off by default): OpenAI text-to-speech (`gpt-4o-mini-tts`), called straight from the phone with the driver's own API key under OpenAI's terms. Nothing is bundled; the audio is made on request and cached on the phone. OpenAI voices are AI-generated and are disclosed as such.
- **Co-driver voices** (`public/voices/*.mp3`): single words recorded with the Kokoro-82M text-to-speech model (Apache License 2.0, commercial use allowed; https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) through kokoro-onnx (MIT; https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx). Voices: bm_george, am_michael, bf_emma, af_heart. The words are generated by `scripts/make-voice-pack.py` and joined into calls on the device; the phone's own voice is the fallback.
