# Growth and revenue (hypotheses to test, not forecasts)

No numbers here are measured. Each idea names the test that would confirm it.

## Why people might share it
The signature element is the road sketch for every corner, drawn from real geometry, plus a single "twist" number for a road. That is a shareable object: "this road is a 91".
1. **Road cards.** One tap makes an image or link for a road with its twist score, hairpin count and the first three calls. Test: share rate per planned route.
2. **Share links already work** (waypoints in the URL hash, no account). Test: percentage of visits that arrive via a shared link and start a ride.
3. **Short video.** The demo drive screen recorded at 2× is the natural clip: big call, glyph, speed. Test: views per clip on a handful of famous roads.

## Ways to charge (pick one to test first)
| Model | What is paid for | Risk |
|-------|------------------|------|
| One-time "Pro" unlock (about the price of a coffee) | Offline stages, saved routes, extra voices, stage-day tools | Needs native stores or a payment page |
| Road packs | Curated pacenote packs for famous roads and tour events | Content effort, licensing of any third-party material |
| Club / event licence | Branded stages for track-day organisers | Sales effort |
| Free app, sponsor | Partner (tyres, insurance) | Trust and safety optics |
Recommended first test: a free web app with a waitlist for Pro, measured by sign-ups per 100 planned routes, before building any payment flow.

## Costs to plan for
Public routing and search services are demos. Self-hosting Valhalla and Photon is the main running cost once traffic is real (a small VPS to start; Valhalla needs a regional OSM extract).
Apple Developer Program (paid) is needed for App Store release; Google Play has a one-time fee. Check current prices before committing.
