Privacy policy

Updated 25 November 2026

Draft. This text describes what the app does today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Highlighted brackets are blanks the owner must fill in before the app is offered in a store.

The short version

  • Navi has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It sets no cookies of its own.
  • Your settings, saved places, history, saved roads and best score stay on your phone.
  • Navi has no server of its own. To work, it asks open map services, and those services can see your internet address and the details listed below.

What stays on your phone

  • Settings (voice, units, vehicle, theme, optional keys and addresses you type in).
  • Saved places, favourites, recent searches and trips.
  • Voice corrections you make, and your best score in the practice game.
  • Saved road shapes for offline use, and voice audio the phone has made or downloaded.

It is held in the browser's storage on your phone. You can back it up or erase it all from Settings, then About and legal, then "Erase everything on this phone".

Your location

Navi asks your phone for your location only when you use a feature that needs it: Free roam, Near me, Fun loop, planning from where you are, and the live co-driver while you drive. Your phone asks you first. If you say no, Navi still works for planning from a start point you search for, but Free roam cannot work.

Location is used on the phone to draw the map and call the road. Parts of it are sent to the services below when a feature needs them.

Each of these services also receives your internet address and the details your browser sends with any request. Navi does not send them a name, an account, an email or a device identifier, because it has none to send.

Optional services, off by default

  • OpenAI voice: if you paste your own OpenAI key, the text of the calls is sent to OpenAI to be spoken. Your key stays on your phone.
  • Traffic (TomTom or HERE): if you add your own key, points along your route are sent to that provider.
  • Your own PC voice: if you connect one, call text goes to the address you gave.

Saved roads and the open map licence

Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence. Saved roads are kept on your phone. Downloading them asks the public Overpass servers for the area you chose.

How long things are kept

Navi keeps nothing on a server. Data on your phone stays until you erase it or clear the browser's storage. The services above keep what they receive under their own policies, for example the OpenStreetMap Foundation, OpenAI, Hugging Face and GitHub.

Your choices

  • Say no to location, and plan from a start point instead.
  • Do not turn on the optional services.
  • Back up or erase everything from Settings.
  • Write to [ADD CONTACT EMAIL] with any question about this policy. Navi holds no personal file about you, so there is nothing to look up, correct or delete on our side.

Children

Navi is a driving tool. It is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If what Navi sends or keeps changes, this page changes first and the date at the top moves. Adding any account, advertising or analytics would need a new version of this policy and a clear request for your consent.
